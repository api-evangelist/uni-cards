# Uni Cards

> Uni Cards (Uniorbit Technologies) is a Bangalore-based Indian consumer fintech, backed by Accel, that builds credit-card and pay-later products — including the Uni Pay 1/3rd Card and co-branded credit cards — delivered through its consumer mobile app. Uni Cards does not publish a public developer portal, API documentation, or client SDKs; api.uni.cards and docs.uni.cards resolve but are closed behind a Cloudflare challenge.
